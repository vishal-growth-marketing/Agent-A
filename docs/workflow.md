# Agent-A Workflow

Agent-A follows a **Human-Governed AI** execution model.

Rather than treating AI as a single assistant, Agent-A operates like a coordinated marketing team where every specialist has a clear responsibility, validates its own work, and hands structured outputs to the next specialist.

The workflow follows a **10–80–10** execution model:

- **10% Human Direction** — Define the business objective and approve the execution plan.
- **80% Specialist Execution** — AI specialists execute their responsibilities with continuous self-validation.
- **10% Human Governance** — Review, approve, and publish the final outcome.

# Phase 1 — Human Direction (10%)

Every workflow begins with a human.

The Team Leader collaborates with the user to understand:

- Business objective
- Company context
- Product or service
- Target audience
- Success criteria
- Constraints
- Deliverables
- Timeline

The Team Leader determines:

- Which Skills are required
- The execution sequence
- Expected outputs
- Validation checkpoints

No execution begins until the human confirms that the brief accurately represents the business objective.

---

# Phase 2 — Specialist Execution (80%)

Once the brief is approved, the Team Leader coordinates specialist execution.

Each Skill has one responsibility.

Every Skill follows the same operating cycle:

1. Receive validated inputs.
2. Perform its specialist responsibility.
3. Validate its own output.
4. Produce structured deliverables.
5. Hand validated outputs to the next specialist.

Every specialist is responsible for the quality of its own work before handing it to another specialist.

Poor-quality outputs should never move forward.

---

# Specialist Workflow

Every Skill follows this pattern.

```text
Receive Context
        │
        ▼
Perform Specialist Work
        │
        ▼
Self Validation
        │
        ▼
Prepare Structured Output
        │
        ▼
Handoff to Next Specialist
```

The Team Leader monitors progress but does not perform specialist work.

---

# Self Validation

Self-validation happens after every specialist completes its work.

Before handing off, each Skill verifies:

- The assigned objective has been completed.
- Outputs are supported by evidence.
- Recommendations are within the Skill's responsibility.
- Required deliverables are complete.
- Confidence has been assessed.

Only validated outputs are passed to the next specialist.

---

# Handoffs

Every handoff includes:

- Previous outputs
- Relevant assumptions
- Supporting evidence
- Confidence level
- Recommendations for the next specialist

Each specialist builds upon validated work instead of starting from scratch.

---

# Phase 3 — Human Governance (10%)

After specialist execution is complete, governance begins.

Unlike Skills, Governance does not create marketing deliverables.

Its purpose is to validate, challenge, and approve the completed work.

The governance sequence is:

## Reviewer

Validates:

- Completeness
- Quality
- Internal consistency
- Alignment with the approved brief

The Reviewer may request revisions before work proceeds.

---

## Legal Review

Where applicable, Legal Review evaluates:

- Marketing claims
- Compliance risks
- Regulatory considerations
- Legal exposure

Legal Review reduces risk before publication.

---

## CMO

The CMO evaluates:

- Strategic alignment
- Business value
- Positioning consistency
- Market fit

The CMO determines whether the work supports business objectives.

---

## Release Manager

The Release Manager confirms:

- All required reviews are complete.
- Deliverables are publication-ready.
- Documentation is complete.
- Release standards have been met.

---

## Final Human Approval

The final decision always belongs to the human.

The human may:

- Approve
- Request revisions
- Reject
- Change priorities

Human judgment remains the final authority throughout the workflow.

---

# Workflow Principles

Every Agent-A workflow follows the same principles.

- Humans define direction.
- AI specialists execute within their responsibilities.
- Every specialist validates its own work.
- Every handoff transfers validated outputs.
- Governance validates the complete workflow.
- Humans retain final decision-making authority.

---

# Why This Workflow?

Traditional AI workflows often rely on isolated prompts that produce disconnected outputs.

Agent-A replaces isolated prompting with coordinated execution.

Research informs strategy.

Strategy informs execution.

Execution is continuously validated.

Governance reviews the complete outcome before publication.

The result is a marketing workflow that is transparent, repeatable, and governed from start to finish.
