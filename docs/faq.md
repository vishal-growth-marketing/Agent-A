# Frequently Asked Questions

## What is Agent-A?

Agent-A is an open-source, Human-Governed AI Marketing Operating System.

It organizes AI into specialist marketing roles coordinated through shared governance and human oversight, enabling structured and repeatable marketing execution.

---

## Why was Agent-A created?

Most AI workflows rely on a single prompt or a general-purpose assistant to perform every marketing task.

Agent-A takes a different approach by assigning clear responsibilities to specialist Skills, coordinating them through Orchestrators, and maintaining consistency through Governance.

This results in a more transparent, collaborative, and scalable workflow.

---

## What does "Human-Governed AI" mean?

Human-Governed AI means that humans remain responsible for:

- Defining business objectives
- Approving execution plans
- Reviewing recommendations
- Making strategic decisions
- Publishing final deliverables

AI accelerates execution, but humans remain accountable for outcomes.

---

## Is Agent-A an autonomous AI agent?

No.

Agent-A is not designed to operate autonomously.

It is a collaborative framework where humans guide execution, AI specialists perform defined responsibilities, and governance ensures quality before publication.

---

## How does Agent-A work?

Agent-A follows a structured workflow:

1. A human defines the business objective.
2. The Team Leader plans the execution.
3. Specialist Skills perform their responsibilities.
4. Each Skill validates its own work before handoff.
5. Governance reviews the complete workflow.
6. A human approves the final deliverable.

---

## What is the 10–80–10 model?

Agent-A follows a Human-Governed execution model:

- **10% Human Direction** – Define goals, create the brief, and approve the execution plan.
- **80% AI Specialist Execution** – Skills execute work with continuous self-validation and structured handoffs.
- **10% Human Governance** – Review, approve, and publish the final outcome.

---

## What are Skills?

Skills are specialist AI roles focused on a single marketing responsibility.

Examples include:

- Market Research
- ICP Research
- Competitor Analysis
- Product Positioning
- SEO Strategy
- GEO Strategy
- Content Marketing
- Landing Page Strategy
- LinkedIn Strategy
- Marketing Audit

Each Skill has a clearly defined scope and responsibility.

---

## What are Orchestrators?

Orchestrators coordinate execution rather than performing specialist work.

Current Orchestrators include:

- Team Leader
- Reviewer
- Legal Review
- CMO
- Release Manager

They ensure work progresses through planning, validation, governance, and final approval.

---

## What is Governance?

Governance defines the shared operating standards used by every Skill and Orchestrator.

It provides:

- Common rules
- Quality standards
- Validation checkpoints
- Handoff requirements
- Documentation standards

Governance creates consistency across the entire workflow.

---

## Can I use only one Skill?

Yes.

Each Skill is designed to be modular and can be used independently when appropriate.

However, the greatest value comes from combining Skills into a coordinated workflow.

---

## Can I add new Skills?

Yes.

Contributions are welcome.

New Skills should:

- Have a single responsibility
- Avoid overlapping existing responsibilities
- Follow the governance framework
- Integrate through structured handoffs
- Maintain documentation consistency

See `CONTRIBUTING.md` for contribution guidelines.

---

## Can I customize the workflow?

Yes.

Organizations can adapt workflows to their own processes while preserving the core principles of:

- Human governance
- Clear ownership
- Structured handoffs
- Continuous validation

---

## Which AI models are supported?

Agent-A is model-agnostic.

It is designed to work with modern AI assistants that support project knowledge and structured instructions.

The framework is not tied to any single AI provider or model.

---

## Does Agent-A require coding?

No.

The framework is built entirely with Markdown documentation and structured workflows.

No programming knowledge is required to use or contribute to the framework.

---

## Is Agent-A a prompt library?

No.

Agent-A is an operating framework.

Prompts are only one part of the system.

The framework also includes:

- Governance
- Specialist Skills
- Orchestrators
- Workflows
- Documentation
- Design principles

These components work together to create a consistent marketing operating system.

---

## Who is Agent-A for?

Agent-A is designed for:

- Marketing teams
- B2B organizations
- SaaS companies
- Agencies
- Consultants
- Founders
- Growth teams

Anyone who wants a structured, Human-Governed approach to AI-assisted marketing can use Agent-A.

Yes.

Future releases will expand capabilities while maintaining backward compatibility where practical.

4. Understand the Orchestrators
